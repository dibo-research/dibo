# Amendment to DIBO Evidence Coding Reliability Pilot 001

Status:

**SUPERSEDED BEFORE CONDUCT**

## Purpose of this amendment

The design preregistered in `PILOT-001.md` required two independent human coders. That design cannot be conducted because two human coders are not available.

Pilot 001 was superseded before data collection. No coder sheet was created, no evidence code was assigned, no reliability result was calculated, and no adjudication occurred under the original design.

The original preregistration remains unchanged as a historical record. This amendment does not reinterpret it as completed work.

## Design change

The two-human-coder inter-rater reliability pilot is cancelled. It is replaced by the separately registered `AUTOMATION-001.md`, a single-operator, single-model automation feasibility run.

The replacement is not an inter-rater reliability study. It will not report human inter-rater reliability, model-to-model agreement, pooled agreement, or Cohen's kappa.

The following original requirements do not carry forward:

- two human coders identified as `C01` and `C02`;
- independent human categorical coding;
- the prohibition on generative AI during coding;
- inter-rater reliability outputs;
- the two-coder reliability and adjudication workflow.

The following boundaries do carry forward:

- Evidence Coding Protocol v0.1 and Codebook v0.1;
- Pilot Kit v0.1 at tag `evidence-pilot-kit-v0.1`;
- the frozen Assessment Scope and neutral source-packet exclusions;
- no additional source discovery during coding;
- separation of generated evidence codes from any later review;
- no Institutional Runaround classification or institutional performance inference.

## Interpretation

No result from the replacement run may be described as evidence of human inter-rater reliability. A future human reliability study would require a new preregistration and two independent human coders.
