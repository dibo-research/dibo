# DIBO-JP-0001 — Former Eugenic Protection Law: state responsibility and compensation

## Scope

This registered lineage covers the contemporary institutional path through which harms caused under the former Eugenic Protection Law moved through litigation, legislation, executive response, compensation legislation, and administrative implementation. It begins with the first state-compensation action filed in Sendai on 2018-01-30. The law's earlier history is relevant background but is outside the registered Episode boundary.

## Why this is one Issue Lineage

The judicial, legislative, and administrative Episodes concern the same underlying public problem: institutional responsibility for harms associated with the former law and the availability of effective redress. The pathways differ, but each addresses recognition, compensation, settlement, or implementation for affected people and eligible family members.

## Timeline

- 2018-01-30 — `J` — A State Redress Act damages action was filed in Sendai District Court.
- 2019-04-24 — `L` — The Diet enacted the first national lump-sum payment law.
- 2019-04-24 — `A` — The national and prefectural implementation process began.
- 2024-07-03 — `J` — The Supreme Court Grand Bench ruled on constitutionality, legislative unlawfulness, and the government's exclusion-period defense.
- 2024-07-17 — `A` — The Prime Minister apologized and stated directions for settlements and a new compensation framework.
- 2024-10-08 — `L` — The Diet unanimously passed comprehensive compensation legislation.
- 2025-01-17 — `A` — The new compensation scheme took effect and claim reception began.

## Status

The compensation scheme remains operational. Official data through 2026-04 reported 2,616 claims and 1,848 recognitions, and the compensation and eugenic-surgery recognition subcommittee continued meeting through 2026-07-16.
