# Licensing Status

No blanket open-source or open-data license has been adopted for this repository.

Licensing is intentionally pending because DIBO may contain different classes of material that require separate treatment:

- software or code;
- original documentation and research protocols;
- structured observational data; and
- links or references to third-party source materials.

Third-party source material remains subject to its original copyright, licensing terms, access conditions, and other applicable restrictions. A link or citation in DIBO does not transfer rights in that material.

The absence of a license does not permit unrestricted copying, modification, redistribution, or reuse. Prospective users should obtain permission where required and await explicit licensing terms for each class of repository content.

Licensing decisions remain unresolved during the pilot phase and will be documented here when adopted.
